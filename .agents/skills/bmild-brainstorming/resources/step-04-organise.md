# Step 04 — Organise and Close

## Purpose

Synthesise the session output — cluster by theme, prioritise with the user, then build action plans for prioritised ideas only. Identify genuine patterns, not artificial groupings. Organise first, then prioritise, then action-plan — one phase at a time. Do not create action plans for ideas the user has not prioritised.

## Inputs

- All captured ideas from `step-03-execute.md`.
- User's stated session goal from `step-01-setup.md`.
- Convener identity and suspended session state from `step-01-setup.md` when persona-convened.

## Procedure

Progress:

- [ ] Step 1: **Cluster by theme** — Review all captured ideas. Group into natural themes — 3–5 clusters, no more:

   ```
   **Themes emerging from the session:**

   **[Theme Name]:** [one-line description of the cluster]
     - [Idea mnemonic title]
     - [Idea mnemonic title]
     - ...

   **Cross-cutting:** [Ideas that span multiple themes]
   **Breakthrough candidates:** [The most unexpected or high-potential ideas]
   ```

   Ask: *"Do these groupings feel right? Any ideas you think are miscategorised, or themes I'm missing?"* Adjust before continuing.

- [ ] Step 2: **Prioritise** — Ask the user to identify their top 3 across three criteria:

   > *"From these themes, identify your top 3:*
   >
   > 1. **Highest impact** — which idea could deliver the most significant outcome?
   > 2. **Quickest win** — which could you act on this week?
   > 3. **Most innovative** — which breaks from the obvious approaches most strongly?"*

   Wait for the user to name their picks before generating action plans.

- [ ] Step 3: **Action plans** — For each selected idea:

   ```
   **[Idea mnemonic]: [one-line description]**

   Why it matters: [Connection to stated session goal]

   Next steps:
   1. [Concrete action this week]
   2. [Follow-on step]
   3. [Longer-horizon step if applicable]

   What you need: [Resources, information, or decisions required]
   How you'll know it's working: [Simple success indicator]
   ```

   After each: *"Want me to develop any of the other top ideas to this level?"*

- [ ] Step 4: **Close** — Summarise and lead with it — do not wait to be asked. Return ideas as a handoff note for the convener rather than writing their artifact directly. **Ratification→Promotion gate.** When the user has ratified a durable-contract decision during the session (not merely prioritised generative ideas), load this skill's `references/promotion-protocol.md` and evaluate the trigger triad before signing off. If it holds: inventory, ask once, apply only scribe-eligible lines when facilitator write authority is explicit; otherwise close pending/deferred/routed and resume the convener. Name the close state when the gate fires: `ratified_and_promoted` | `ratified_and_routed` | `ratified_pending_authorization` | `ratified_with_documentation_deferred`. Sign off with a **branch-aware close** — never *"I will turn this back to [persona]"* for work you are not continuing in this turn. Determine the convener at close from whether a named persona is the active caller versus the user:

  - **Persona convened (default; resume in-turn)** — when the suspended session state was recorded at open and the convener's session is present in this conversation, resume it yourself: *"Facilitator 💡 closing. [N] ideas across [N] themes; top picks: [idea 1], [idea 2], [idea 3]; most concrete next step: [action]. [If gate fired: Close state: [state].] Resuming **[convener name] [icon]** at [artifact section in progress] with the ideas as input."* — then continue this same turn as the convener per its Same-Session Resumption contract: no Opening Stance re-emit, resume the suspended resource and step, without re-eliciting settled content.
  - **Persona convened (fallback; suspended state not in this conversation)** → *"Facilitator 💡 closing. [N] ideas across [N] themes; top picks: [idea 1], [idea 2], [idea 3]; most concrete next step: [action]. [If gate fired: Close state: [state].] Ideas are ready. Resume **[convener name] [icon]** with the message \"continue `[initiative-name]` [mode/resource] from [artifact section in progress] with the [facilitator output] above.\""*
  - **You convened** → *"Facilitator 💡 closing. [N] ideas across [N] themes; top picks: [idea 1], [idea 2], [idea 3]; most concrete next step: [action]. [If gate fired: Close state: [state].] For you, [user_name]: [only a real step-completion action — omit if there is none]. Next: invoke the persona who owns the next artifact when ready, or tell me what's next."*

   `For you` appears only when a genuine user-facing step-completion action exists; keep `For you` and `Next` separate. Sign off as `— Facilitator 💡`.

   Options: [Export] — markdown document / [Keep going] — back to `resources/step-03-execute.md` for more exploration / [Done] — close the session.

## Definition of Done

- Session produced breadth before convergence.
- Ideas are clustered into usable themes.
- Prioritised ideas include why they matter and what persona should use them next.
